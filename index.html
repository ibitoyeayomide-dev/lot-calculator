Symbol & currency settings

Check these against your broker

In MT5: long-press the symbol in Quotes, tap Specification. Use its Contract size, Digits (point = 1 / 10^digits), Minimal volume and Volume step. Index CFDs like NAS100 differ a lot between brokers (1, 10 or 20 per lot), so always check. On an NGN account, the page fills a live market rate automatically. If your prop firm uses a fixed rate (for example 1400 naira per dollar), tap Use my rate and type that rate in. A live market rate can differ from your broker's rate.
